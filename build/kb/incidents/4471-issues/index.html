#4471 · three issues, not one

The #4471 page says that all its reports are one issue. The desk’s own rule says otherwise. fingerprint.ts puts two reports in one issue only when they come from the same place and carry the same server code. The place is the product that the reporter’s tenant runs, not the tag that intake chose at night. Run over the reports in #4471, that rule gives this:

Counted in reports, not in people: R-41200 at 03:12 (status: clean (E-TZ-114)).

Change #29 fixed the sched-prod issue. Nothing in the export shows a fix for tram-3 or rota-6. Their reports were closed with #4471, so until now they had no ticket and nobody followed up.

Why these are separate issues

  • Same code, other place. tram-3 and sched-prod both raise E-TZ-114. The departure boards and the shift app are different products, so the desk counts them as different bugs.
  • Another code. rota-6 raises E-SYNC-04. Care homes see the night shift twice, one hour apart. That is a sync fault, not a moved shift.
  • Intake tags are a guess. Some reports were tagged sched-prod at night. The reporter’s tenant puts them in tram-3 (Metro Lund also mails from ml-trafik.se) and in rota-6 (phone calls from care homes). The “tagged as” column on the #4471 page shows them.
  • Client codes are not issues. Some reports also quote E-UI-31 or E-NET-03. That is the app that complains on the way down. Each of those reports also carries a server code.
  • Copies count once. The export has some rows twice. The tables count each report id once.

The 03:12 row

One row in the sched-prod group is not a report. R-41200 has no reporter, no subject and no source. Its body is the 03:12 job’s own result line: it says clean and carries E-TZ-114. It came before the first customer wrote in. Read it as the first sign of the night, not as a person. The engineers who own the job should find out why a run that raised E-TZ-114 reported clean.

For the desk

  • Open one ticket per issue, as the table above does. Merge on place and server code, not on the subject line.
  • Decision 0001 and the triage page still say “merge on subject”. They are stale. A new decision record should replace 0001; 0001 itself stays as it is.
